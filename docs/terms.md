# Terms of Use

> **Not reviewed by a lawyer.** This text describes the terms the app is designed around.

_Last updated: 2026-11-08_

## What Hashwall is

Hashwall lets you block, allow and name phone numbers on your iPhone, and optionally use a
community spam list that is maintained in the open.

## Free features

Blocking, allowing and naming your own numbers and ranges is free and does not require a
purchase.

## Free trial and unlock

For the first 30 days after you first install Hashwall, you can follow, update and import lists
and report spam for free. There is nothing to cancel, and you are never charged automatically.

> _As of today, reporting a number opens a GitHub Issue that only this project's maintainers
> can actually file; the general public can't yet submit a report this way. See the
> [repository README](https://github.com/g4bcloud/hashwall-data#reporting)._

After the trial, a **one-time purchase** ("Unlock") keeps these features. It is not a
subscription. If you don't buy it, the lists you downloaded keep blocking calls, but they stop
updating, and you can't add lists or send reports.

The unlock covers the app as long as it is available. Hashwall is a small independent project
and may stop being offered on the App Store; an app you already installed may keep working for a
while after that, but it won't be updated.

Payment, taxes and refunds are handled by Apple under the App Store terms.

## No guarantee

Hashwall is provided "as is". No spam filter is perfect:

- Some unwanted calls may not be blocked.
- A wanted call may be blocked by mistake, for example if a number was wrongly reported.
  Add the number to your Allow list to make sure it always rings through.

The community list is built from reports by other people. It may contain errors.

## Acceptable use

Do not submit reports in bad faith, for example to get someone's legitimate number blocked, or
automate reports. Such reports may be removed.

## Public lists

The community list and the rules that build it are published at
<https://github.com/g4bcloud/hashwall-data>. The Hashwall app itself is not open source.

Anyone can publish a list for others to follow. Lists are created and maintained by third
parties, not by Hashwall, except for the Hashwall community list. We don't check other lists and
aren't responsible for them. You can unfollow a list at any time, and your Allow list always
overrides any list.

## Contact

Questions about these terms go to the project's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/g4bcloud/hashwall-data/issues).
